College isn't useless

May 21, 2026

I've heard that college is useless now because of the job market and other stuff.

"Your degree is just a piece of paper", "It won't help you land a job now", "you can literally learn the things in college with AI", "college is a scam", "just take a bootcamp", blah blah blah

My parents told me that the greatest gift is education, and I've been told that taking college is normal and that stuff. They don't encourage me to dropout compared to Mark Zuckerberg and Elon Musk who became ambitious and built $1B+ companies.

Here is where I believe college is useful:

  • Most of the jobs require a college degree, especially for more corporate jobs : I believe that the reason for getting a college degree is useful is because it will help me land a job. I'm not saying having a degree instantly lands you a job, but I'm saying you'll meet a core qualification most job postings ask for at the very least and that you likely won't be cut out.
  • You learn fundamentals: People say college is useless because their curriculum is outdated and information evolves. You can literally build with AI and use ChatGPT to learn faster and get instant answers. I'm talking from a computer science and AI standpoint. Learning the fundamentals is the foundation which doesn't change. Technologies evolve, but fundamentals don't. Also knowing the fundamentals at xyz makes you a better validator when it comes to analyzing ChatGPT's answers, making you stand out from other people who are naive and believe everything AI generates is always right.
  • Networking: In college, you get to meet new people, join clubs and organizations, events, career fairs, etc. This is an important part since networking can land you opportunities and build strong connections.

Ultimately, people are saying college is useless and a scam, and we get this notion that people should dropout and make a billion dollar company instead. But here's the thing, people who say that aren't making the most out of it and are blind to the benefits. I think it costs a lot of money, but it'll be worth the money if you make the most of your college's resources and benefits.

Staying at University of Michigan Dearborn

I go to University of Michigan Dearborn, a mid-size campus, mostly commute, non-target school. I believe there's little opportunites at least from my perspective. There's aren't a lot of ambitious, cracked builders. The organizations are small. But through it all, I still try to make the most out of my campus's resources.

To be fair I wanted to transfer to Ann Arbor, but I felt purposeless and felt it might be better if I stayed at Dearborn. Given my confidence and my decent brand, I believed people needed me eventually, and I want to make a change in my campus. It starts with being a technical lead for Google Developer Groups on Campus at UM-Dearborn starting next fall.

I plan to finish my Bachelor's degree at UM-Dearborn, and pursuing my Master's some place else. I could be pursuing my master's here, but going to a different university means different life, more connections, and more opportunites
